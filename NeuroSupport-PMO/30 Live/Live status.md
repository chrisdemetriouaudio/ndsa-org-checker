> **Proof or evidence:** evidence comes from the website and can be wrong. Proof comes from an official record or the organisation itself.
> **Closure is only "proven"** when a proof category is 🔴. Website evidence alone means "likely closed".
>
> - **Web address** (evidence, 15 points): 🔴 means likely closed.
> - **Website answers** (evidence, 15 points): 🔴 means needs a check.

> - **Not for sale** (evidence, 15 points): 🔴 means likely closed.
> - **Same address** (evidence, 5 points): 🔴 means needs a check.
> - **Charity register** (proof, 25 points): the official charity record. 🔴 removed or dissolved proves closure; 🔴 insolvent or in administration means likely closed.
> - **Confirmed by them** (proof, 25 points): the organisation replies itself.
